About Zerox Technology

At Zerox Technology, we provide engineering and construction solutions across the construction and mining industries, specializing in bulk storage tank construction, civil engineering, and general supply services. Fully registered and compliant with all regulatory bodies, we are known for reliable delivery and precision on large-scale industrial projects.

Operating from Lusaka and Solwezi, Zerox serves clients across Zambia by delivering cost-effective, turnkey solutions backed by skilled expertise, modern equipment, strict safety compliance, and internationally recognized quality standards.
